Findaro vs Algolia, Typesense, Meilisearch and others

An honest comparison, including where the others are ahead today. Checked on 17 October 2026 from each vendor's own pricing and docs pages (sources at the bottom). Prices and features change, so check the links before you decide. Spotted a mistake? Open an issue and we'll fix it.

When to choose what

Choose Findaro if

  • Your shoppers type the way they talk, in Indian English or Hinglish (“kaale joote 1000 ke andar”)
  • You want filters understood from the query and shown back as chips shoppers can remove
  • You want a chat shopping assistant (AI Mode) without building RAG plumbing yourself
  • You want open source you can self-host, or flat pricing in rupees
  • Your catalog has up to about 100,000 products

Choose something else if

  • You need a search analytics dashboard and merchandising rules today (Algolia, Boost, Shopify)
  • You want a one-click Shopify app (Shopify Search & Discovery, Boost, Algolia)
  • You need autocomplete, recommendations or personalization out of the box (Algolia)
  • You need a contractual SLA and enterprise compliance now (Algolia, Elastic)
  • You want a general-purpose search engine to build everything on yourself (Typesense, Meilisearch, OpenSearch)

One by one

vs Algolia

Algolia is ahead on scale, analytics, merchandising, personalization, recommendations and ecosystem. It is the safe default for large teams.

Findaro differs: open source and self-hostable; flat ₹ pricing instead of per-request billing; query understanding and chat in the base product, where Algolia's NeuralSearch is on its custom-priced Elevate plan and Agent Studio is in beta; Hinglish built in.

vs Typesense

Typesense is ahead on maturity as a general search engine, speed and community. It also offers natural-language search and a conversational RAG API.

Findaro differs: a product, not building blocks. Its natural-language search sends queries to an LLM you configure; Findaro reads most queries with rules and only asks a model about words it can't place. Findaro ships the shopping UI (chips, AI Mode, compare). Apache-2.0 instead of GPL-3.0.

vs Meilisearch

Meilisearch is ahead on developer experience, a polished cloud from $20/mo, and hybrid search across many languages.

Findaro differs: commerce-specific understanding (prices, attributes, categories inferred from your own data, shown as chips), Hinglish, and a finished chat shopping experience where Meilisearch's chat endpoint is experimental and leaves the UI to you.

vs Elasticsearch / OpenSearch

They are ahead on raw scale, tooling and observability. Findaro itself runs on OpenSearch.

Findaro differs: you get query understanding, relaxation, chat and a widget on top, instead of building them. Elastic's ELSER model is recommended for English.

vs Shopify Search & Discovery

It is ahead on price (free), one-click setup and built-in analytics for Shopify stores.

Findaro differs: works on any platform through an API or a script tag; Hinglish (Hindi isn't in its language list); conversational AI Mode and grounded product Q&A, which the app doesn't have. No Shopify app yet.

vs Boost, SearchTap and other store apps

They are ahead on filters, merchandising campaigns, reports and platform integrations (Boost from $29/mo on Shopify; SearchTap plans from $59/mo).

Findaro differs: natural-language understanding with visible chips, Hinglish, chat shopping and an open-source core. We found no natural-language or Hinglish claims on their pages.
