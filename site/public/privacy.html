Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. What the hosted Findaro beta collects, why, and what we do with it.

Short version: we process your catalog, your users' search queries and what you send us through the contact form, only to run the Service and talk to you. We don't sell data. The hosted Service runs in Oracle Cloud's Mumbai (India) region.

Who is responsible

__COMPANY_NAME__ (“we”) operates the hosted Findaro service and this website. For the catalog data and search queries processed through the Service on your behalf, you are in control of that data and we process it on your instructions. For contact form data, we are responsible for it.

What we process

DataWhat it isWhy
Catalog dataThe product documents you import: titles, descriptions, prices, attributes, image and product URLs.To index it and answer searches and chat questions about it.
Search queries and chat messagesWhat your users type into search or the AI chat, plus the results and answers returned. Chat conversations are kept in a short-lived session (about 30 minutes) so follow-up questions work.To return results and answers, to keep the Service working (debugging, abuse prevention, rate limiting) and to improve search quality for your project.
Technical dataIP address, request origin, user agent, timestamps, API key used. Security, rate limiting, checking allowed origins, and troubleshooting.
Contact form dataName, work email, company, website, catalog size, plan interest and your message.To reply to you and set up your account.

The Service is meant for product data. Please don't put personal data about individuals in your catalog. End users can type anything into a search box, so treat queries as potentially personal and tell your users in your own privacy notice that search is provided by a third party.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent data, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • We don't use your catalog or your users' queries for any other customer.
  • This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It only stores your currency choice (INR or USD) in your browser.

Where data is stored

The hosted Service and its databases run in Oracle Cloud Infrastructure's Mumbai (India) region.

AI features use language models to understand some queries and write chat answers. Depending on configuration, these models run on our own servers or are provided by a third-party AI provider. If a third-party provider is used, we send it only the text needed for that answer (the query, or the question and the products on screen), and we'll tell you which provider on request.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the Service, such as our cloud host (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure) and an email provider to reply to you. They process data only on our instructions. We'll give you the current list on request.

How long we keep it

  • Catalog data: until you delete it through the API or your account ends. After that we delete it from the Service, and from backups as they expire.
  • Search and chat logs: only as long as needed for the purposes above.
  • Contact form data: as long as needed to talk to you and manage your account.

Security

API keys are stored hashed. Admin keys are refused from browsers, and search keys only work from the origins you allow. Traffic to the API is encrypted in transit.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, as provided by applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Your end users can contact you; we'll help you respond.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell customers by email.

Contact

Use the contact form and mention “privacy” in your message.
